### Corporate Structure

- **Holding Company**: Cruise Yacht Upper Holdco Ltd (Malta)
- **US Operating Entity**: Cruise Yacht Holdco Ltd (Fort Lauderdale, FL)
- **Ownership**: Oaktree Capital Management (55%), Mohari Hospitality/Scheinberg family (30%), GIC Singapore (15%)
- **Marriott Relationship**: The Ritz-Carlton Hotel Company (Marriott subsidiary) provides hospitality services under a long-term operating agreement — NOT an equity owner

### Crew vs Shoreside Employment

| Category | Employer | Benefits Filing |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| **Shoreside Staff** | Cruise Yacht Holdco Ltd (Fort Lauderdale) | Files US Form 5500 (371 welfare participants) |
| **Shipboard Crew** | Employed via V.Ships Leisure (offshore manning agency) | No US Form 5500 (offshore employment) |

V.Ships Leisure is the **exclusive recruitment partner** for deck, engine, and hotel crew. Ship crew are employed under maritime law through this offshore manning agency, so they do not appear in US benefit plan filings.

### Observations

1. **Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection** has the smallest shoreside operation (371 participants) but significant premium per participant (~$5,600) reflecting luxury coverage
2. **Royal Caribbean** is the only cruise line with explicit stop-loss coverage on Schedule A ($1.14M)
3. **Lockton** serves both Ritz-Carlton Yacht and Viking
4. **Mercer** serves Carnival and Norwegian
5. **Virgin Voyages** changed broker from Hays Companies to Brown & Brown between 2023-2024
