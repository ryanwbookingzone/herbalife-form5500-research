## 3. DOL filing rules (summary)
- Form 5500 is due on the last day of the 7th month after plan year end (July 31 for calendar-year plans). Form 5558 extends this by 2.5 months (to Oct 15).
- Welfare plans with 100+ participants at the start of the year must file. Small plans that are fully insured or unfunded are exempt.
- Schedule A is required for each insurance contract. It reports broker commissions and fees by person.
- Schedule C is required for large plans and lists service providers paid $5,000 or more.
- Self-funded (ASO) arrangements are not insurance contracts, so they produce no Schedule A. Stop-loss covers the employer and is often reported anyway.
- DOL FOIA datasets are published at askebsa.dol.gov/FOIA Files/{year}/Latest/. The current year's file is partial.
